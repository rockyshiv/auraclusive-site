Our product

Myithri The AI voice assistant for NGOs and sports academies

Myithri talks with the people you serve and turns every conversation into something you can act on and share. It already writes the impact stories of the athletes at the Divyaang Myithri Sports Academy.

  • Voice conversations

    Share one WhatsApp link. Myithri talks with each person by voice and asks your questions in their words.

  • English, Hindi, Kannada

    People answer in the language they think in. Summaries and reports come back in English.

  • Stories and impact reports

    Summaries, themes, follow-ups, designed impact stories and a PDF report for donors and CSR partners.

  • Consent first

    Myithri says it is an AI and asks for consent before every conversation. Your data stays in your own Google Drive.
